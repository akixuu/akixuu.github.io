**EcoConnect** (also known as E-Connect through S.U.C.C.E.S.S. BC) was a
community program where our team budgeted and used funding to introduce more
green spaces to schools. We provided gardening kits and organized activities to
raise awareness around environmental issues. This was made possible thanks to S.U.C.C.E.S.S. BC's [CLI program](https://successbc.ca/cli/).

### Involvement
In our team of 5, I contributed heavily on the ideation, planning, writing, and execution of the project.

### Highlights
- Budgeted and allocated project funding (~$2,000)
- Delivered gardening kits and eco-activities to +7 schools in Vancouver and Richmond
- Focused on youth engagement and sustainability literacy
