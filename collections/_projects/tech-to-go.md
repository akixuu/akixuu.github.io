**Tech To Go** is a community initiative with Tonari Gumi focused on technology
fluency: hands-on workshops, drop-in support, and practical help for folks who
want to feel more comfortable with devices, software, and the web.

### Involvement
I was initially involved as a volunteer from 2022. Tonari Gumi Received government funding $20k to meet the growing demand for more bilingual support services, and I became involved as an Assitant Program Manager from Mar 2024 – Mar 2025 to start up the program.

### Problem
Due to the digitization of many services after Covid-19, may seniors struggled to adapt to this shift. Japanese-speaking seniors in Metro Vancouver lacked accessible technology support due to language barriers and limited opportunities to learn at their own pace.

### What I Worked On
- Helped run hands-on workshops, drop-in sessions, and one-on-one (1:1) tech support
- Troubleshot devices and developed activities/lessons to improve technological fluency
- Coordinated with volunteers to support participants with different levels of digital literacy
- Initiated collaborative opportunities with local organizations and institutions to expand service reach
- Developed curriculums for all levels of technical proficiency – I even helped one senior build their own website!
- Developed all marketing material (to uniquely non-digital audiences), volunteer recruiment campaigns, and website (developed with clear UX/UI principles in mind)
- Wrote the offically submitted final report to the government

### Impact
- **+250** seniors reached across Metro Vancouver, with **+30** workshops and **+70** 1:1 sessions
- **+20** volunteers trained across multiple locations
- Helped seniors build confidence and independence with everyday technology
- Created opportunities for meaningful intergenerational and multicultural connections
- Senior suppport clubs established in local Japanese communities
