Cashier - Izumiya Japanese Marketplace
My first official job! Cashiering is mentally though—you're pretty much stood in place for 8 hours, and either you're overwhelmed by the rush, or there's hardly anyone at all... You were allowed to take almost-expiring food home, tasty! Thank you to the manager for being patient with me. I learnt about resilience, professionalism, and how a smile can go a long way. 

Food Truck Staff - Japadog
My second job. I met a bunch of working-holiday people from all over. They had lots of fun stories. Shoutout to the dude who blew up his school toilet??? Working in the summer was especially fun, staff would buy ice cream for eachother and it was busy all the time! I felt like I was playing Overcooked. Juggling and prioritizing orders was especially challenging in the grueling summertime heat and the tight space of the truck, but teamwork, communication, and good cheer pushed us through the crazy summer rushes.
